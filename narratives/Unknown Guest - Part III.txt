Lambda Core
Unknown Guest Part III

04:00 – Eientei Exterior and Bamboo Forest – Clear Night, Full Moon
First-person perspective – Gameplay Mode

The screen fades in from black.

Freeman stands inside the Eientei guest room, measuring approximately 5 × 5 meters. The room is illuminated only by soft moonlight entering through the partially opened entrance. Behind him, the hidden passage leading back toward the laboratory has already been sealed and is no longer accessible.

The room remains completely silent.

Freeman slowly walks toward the sliding entrance door.

As he approaches, the concealed mechanism activates automatically.

The wooden door quietly slides downward, revealing the outside environment.

Beyond the entrance lies the Bamboo Forest.

Tall bamboo surrounds both sides of the pathway, naturally restricting movement without the need for visible barriers. Dense vegetation fills the surrounding area, while the full moon provides cold blue illumination across the forest floor.

Although the playable area immediately outside Eientei measures approximately 4 × 8 meters, the open space remains completely empty, emphasizing isolation rather than exploration.

Freeman steps outside.

The sliding door behind him quietly closes several seconds later.

A narrow dirt road approximately three meters wide stretches straight ahead.

The terrain is uneven but naturally smooth, with subtle elevation differences throughout the pathway.

On both sides of the road, the surrounding ground rises nearly two meters higher than the walking path.

Numerous bamboo trees occupy the elevated terrain, creating dense natural walls that strongly discourage players from attempting to leave the intended route.

Freeman continues forward.

After approximately twenty meters, the road gently curves toward the right.

Beyond the curve, four large wooden crates completely block the pathway.

Freeman raises his crowbar.

He destroys every crate one by one.

Wooden debris scatters across the ground.

As the final crate breaks apart, a weapon becomes visible beneath the debris.

A Shotgun.

Beside it lies a box containing thirty shotgun shells.

Freeman picks up the weapon.

Freeman continues deeper into the Bamboo Forest.

The road gradually descends, lowering approximately two meters while maintaining a gentle slope.

Fifteen meters ahead, the pathway curves smoothly toward the left.

Freeman rounds the corner.

Five humanoid figures stand motionless approximately five meters ahead.

Their bodies resemble zombies.

However, their appearance constantly flickers with unstable Youkai energy, producing unnatural distortions around their silhouettes.

Without hesitation, Freeman engages them.

The shotgun echoes throughout the forest.

Each distorted creature collapses after taking sufficient damage.

Instead of leaving corpses behind, every defeated enemy rapidly disintegrates into countless glowing particles resembling the disintegration effect produced by the AR2 Combine Energy Ball.

Silence returns.

Freeman reloads.

He continues walking.

Ten meters ahead, the road bends sharply toward the right.

Beyond the bend, the path extends straight for another fifteen meters.

At the end of the road lies another drop.

Approximately 2.5 meters below, another section of the Bamboo Forest continues beneath the elevated pathway.

Freeman carefully steps down.

The moment both feet touch the ground—

Exactly 1.5 seconds later—

The screen instantly fades to black.

Player movement is completely disabled.

No visual information remains.

A heavy impact strikes Freeman from behind.

The sound is violent.

Direct.

Unexpected.

Freeman immediately loses consciousness.

Two seconds pass.

Only distant echoes remain.

The surrounding ambience becomes increasingly muffled, as though heard through fading awareness.

Eirin Yagokoro:
“Interesting.”
「興味深いわ。」

The echo slowly disappears.

Every remaining sound gradually fades into absolute silence.

Five seconds later—

The screen remains completely black.

No sound.

No movement.

Nothing.
