Lambda Core
Subject: Gordon Freeman

Prologue — Unknown Guest

Black screen.

Two seconds later, a title slowly fades into existence.

“Lambda Core”

The logo remains visible for five seconds before dissolving back into darkness.

Three seconds of silence.

A line of text slowly emerges against the black screen.

“It isn’t about survival alone.
It’s about how they treat you when you do.
Every path is yours… including the ones that end in death.”
— Unknown

The text lingers for five seconds before fading away gradually.

Three seconds later.

23:00 — Great Youkai Forest, Clear Night.
First-Person Perspective — Gameplay Mode

The screen remains black.

Three seconds later, a distant echo cuts through the silence.

G-Man:
“Well... it looks like we won't be working together. No regrets, Mr. Freeman.”
「どうやら……我々はもう共に仕事をすることはなさそうですね。後悔はありませんよ、フリーマン氏。」

Four seconds later, reality violently returns.

Moonlight barely penetrates the endless forest ahead.

Gordon Freeman stands alone in the middle of the Great Youkai Forest.

Tall trees stretch endlessly on both sides of the dirt pathway. The terrain is uneven, distorted by small natural hills and subtle elevation shifts that force the camera movement to sway gently with each step. The forest is quiet, but the silence feels unnatural.

Cold wind drifts through the leaves overhead.

The pathway ahead extends nearly fifty meters forward, curving subtly through the darkness. Grass and scattered vegetation grow wildly around the terrain, while groups of trees stand in irregular formations, partially obscured by the pale blue moonlight.

No traps.
No visible danger.

Only silence.

And movement within the trees.

Several distant silhouettes slowly emerge between the forest lines.

Youkai.

Watching.

Following.

Gordon continues walking forward.

The deeper he moves into the forest, the heavier the atmosphere becomes.

After 10 seconds,

The screen suddenly cuts to black.

A violent impact echoes through the forest as Gordon collapses directly onto the ground.

His condition has not recovered.

Three seconds later.

HEV Suit:
“Warning! Vital Signs Critical. Seek Medical Attention.”

The screen remains black.

Five seconds later—

Gordon’s eyes slowly open.

His vision is heavily blurred.

A dark blue figure runs toward him through the forest. From Gordon’s tilted perspective, her silhouette appears slanted against the uneven terrain due to his collapsed position on the ground.

The moonlight briefly reveals her face.

A woman.

As Gordon’s consciousness begins fading again, her voice echoes faintly through the darkness.

Eirin:
“Impossible...”
「あり得ない……」

The sound of distant wind continues flowing through the trees.

Three seconds later, her voice echoes once more, but this time it sounds farther away.

Less clear.

More distorted.

Gordon’s consciousness rapidly deteriorates.

Eirin:
“Doctor... Gordon Freeman?”
「ゴードン・フリーマン……博士？」

The voice slowly disappears into the darkness.

Gordon loses consciousness.

Black screen.

“Loading...”
