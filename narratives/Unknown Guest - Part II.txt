Unknown Guest - Part II

04:00 — Eientei Interior
First-Person Perspective — Gameplay Mode

The screen slowly transitions back to reality.
Gordon Freeman finds himself standing inside a modest room measuring approximately eight by eight meters. The lighting is bright enough to illuminate every corner of the interior. A simple wooden table stands against the left side of the room. Its surface is completely empty.
A plain futon lies beneath Gordon's feet.
Directly ahead stands a wooden door leading outside the room.

Gordon opens it.
A corridor stretches approximately ten meters forward. Three doors line both the left and right sides of the hallway. None of them respond when interacted with.
From Gordon's perspective, the corridor appears roughly three meters wide.

The hallway is noticeably darker than the room behind him.
No voices.
No movement.
Only the distant ambient sounds of Eientei.

Gordon continues forward.
At the end of the corridor, the path turns left, revealing another straight hallway extending approximately ten meters ahead.
The atmosphere remains quiet.
Almost unnaturally so.

Upon reaching the end of the second hallway, the path bends right.
A staircase comes into view.
The incline is gentle, extending approximately fifteen meters upward.

Gordon ascends.
The wooden structure creaks softly beneath his weight.
After reaching the upper floor, a branching corridor becomes visible, leading left and right.

Gordon turns right.
Two doors stand along the corridor.
The door on the left remains locked.
The door on the right opens.

Gordon enters.
Inside, a simple wooden table, a chair, and several pieces of basic wooden equipment occupy the room.

A familiar object rests atop the table.
A crowbar.
The metal surface reflects the room's dim lighting.

Without hesitation, Gordon takes it.
Crowbar acquired.

Leaving the room behind, Gordon returns to the intersection and proceeds toward the left side of the upper floor.
Approximately eight meters later, the corridor reaches another corner.

The path turns right for roughly five meters before bending left once again.
After the final turn, a larger double-door frame becomes visible on the left side.
Gordon enters.
The room beyond measures approximately ten by fifteen meters.
Several pieces of furniture similar to those found throughout Eientei occupy the interior.

However, unlike the previous rooms, laboratory-related equipment becomes increasingly visible.
Glass containers.
Medical instruments.
Research apparatus.

The atmosphere immediately feels different.
More organized.
More purposeful.
At the far end of the room stands another double-door frame identical to the previous one.

Gordon approaches.
Passing through the doorway, he enters what appears to be a small reception room measuring approximately five by five meters.
Simple furnishings occupy the space.
Another familiar door stands directly ahead.

If Gordon failed to retrieve the crowbar from the previous room, attempting to interact with the door causes a loud forcing sound to echo through the area.
The door remains locked.
No amount of effort opens it.
If the crowbar was collected, the path remains accessible.
Gordon proceeds forward.

The moment he enters the area—
"Loading..."
No transition.
No fade.
No warning.

Only an abrupt interruption.
